Neoship × Odoo 19 — shipping guide

01

Before you start

Prerequisites

  • A Neoship account: username and password.
  • At least one carrier activated on the Neoship account. Only active carriers appear in the carrier picker (chapter 02).
  • If you ship with Slovenská pošta, ask the Neoship administrators to disable the automatic daily closure for your account. Otherwise Neoship closes Slovenská pošta shipments on its own; closures from Odoo are described in chapter 10.
  • Test (t-we-nshp-webapp-01-app.azurewebsites.net) and production (aplikacia.neoship.sk) use separate Neoship credentials. The credentials on a delivery method must match its environment: test credentials in the Test Environment, production credentials in production.

The examples you will follow

The shop is Zrnko s.r.o., a coffee roastery at Panónska cesta 7, 851 04 Bratislava, shipping from its warehouse Sklad Bratislava (transfers BA/OUT/…). Prices include 23 % VAT, every order is in EUR, and the e-shop marks the payment with an order tag: Dobierka (cash on delivery) or Platba kartou (paid by card).

02

Connect the account & choose a carrier

  1. Open Inventory a → Configuration b → Delivery Methods c and click New d. Delivery is only a group heading in the menu. Neoship Closure Schedules and Protocols e sit in the same group; read chapter 10 before using them.
  2. Enter a recognisable name, such as “Kuriér SPS”, and set Provider = Neoship b. Keep Test Environment a in the header while learning. Enter the delivery charge in Fixed Price d (here €3.90 including VAT); it is your Odoo price, not a Neoship tariff. Keep Integration Level c at Get Rate and Create Shipment, so that validating a delivery creates the shipment. Continue with step 3.
  3. Open the Neoship Configuration tab (e in step 2), enter the test username and password and click Test Connection f. The green “Connection successful.” confirms the login.
  4. Click Choose (g in step 2) beside Neoship Carrier. The live list shows the carriers active on your account (those with a price list); one Odoo method uses one Neoship carrier. Click Select on SPS a. Packeta b opens a second, country-specific picker (chapter 04). Pickup Points c means the carrier supports pickup points (chapter 05).

    Choosing SPS, Packeta or Slovenská pošta also creates an archived closure schedule a, counted on the Closure Schedules button b; the method now stores the carrier c. Nothing is closed until an administrator activates the schedule (chapter 10).

  5. Choosing the carrier also sets Label Format to the first format Neoship offers for it, here A4. To print on another paper size or on a label printer, click Choose (j in step 2) beside it and click Select on the format a. The formats b come from Neoship and differ per carrier. A shipment keeps the format it was created with, so a change applies to new shipments only. For Packeta, a format marked Pickup Points Only prints only pickup-point shipments; deliveries to an address use the first address format instead.
  6. If a fallback weight is needed, set Neoship Default Weight (h in step 2). The weight comes from Weight for shipping → product weights of the delivery → method default, in that order, converted to kilograms.
  7. Under Cash on Delivery, click Edit Domain (i in step 2) and define which sales orders are COD; here Tags → Tag Name = Dobierka. Save the method.

Only the Cash on Delivery Orders rule decides COD (i in step 2). Odoo's standard Cash on Delivery checkbox next to Provider is unrelated: it lets e-shop customers choose cash-on-delivery payment with this method. The Neoship module does not read it.

Without a condition, nothing is COD. An empty rule and a filter saved without conditions both mean that no order is COD, and a blue “No order is cash on delivery” note appears below the editor. The editor itself still shows “Match all records”; the note is what applies. After setting the rule, send one prepaid and one COD order and check the COD amount.

Rule used in this tutorial: [("tag_ids.name", "=", "Dobierka")]. Use your integration's real payment indicator in production. An invalid rule is rejected when you save the method.

03

Ship the first order with SPS

  1. In Sales → New, select the customer a and use Add a product b to add stocked products c.

    On Other Info a, set the payment tag in Tags b. This order is tagged Dobierka, so the cash on delivery rule from chapter 02 (step 6) makes it COD. Orders from your e-shop get the tag from the integration.

  2. Click Add shipping, choose the delivery method you created in chapter 02 (here Kuriér SPS) a, and click Get rate b. The method's fixed price appears as Cost c; then click Add d. If you click Add while Cost is 0.00, Odoo asks whether delivery should be free — cancel and get the rate first.
  3. Check the shipping line a with the method's price (€3.90) and the total b, €40.60 including VAT, which the courier collects for a COD order. Then click Confirm c.
  4. The quotation becomes a sales order and the Delivery smart button a appears; it opens the delivery order.
  5. On Operations, check that the quantities are what you actually dispatch. On Additional Info, check the carrier a and Weight for shipping b, the packed weight sent to the carrier. Then click Validate c.
  6. On the first validation, Odoo may ask whether to text customers about validated deliveries. Choose Disable SMS a or Confirm; it is Odoo's stock SMS feature, not Neoship, and can also be switched off in the Inventory settings.
  7. The delivery is now Done a: the warehouse transfer is finished, not the delivery to the customer. The chatter records the carrier, tracking number and delivery charge b and holds the label PDF c. Open Additional Info d.
  8. Additional Info shows the tracking reference a and the Neoship status b, which Odoo reads right after sending; Exportovaná (Exported) is separate from Odoo Done. Update Tracking f refreshes it on demand. Every Neoship parcel of the delivery has its own row c with the COD actually sent d: €40.60, products plus shipping. Download Label Again e fetches the same shipment's PDF.
  9. Open the PDF from the chatter and print it with the browser. Check the recipient a against the box; SPS marks the services b (express, COD, phone notification) and the reference c is the delivery BA/OUT/00006.

Validation did not send the shipment? The delivery is Done but has no tracking number. Click Send to Shipper in the delivery's header. For the shipment to be sent automatically on validation, the method's Integration Level must be Get Rate and Create Shipment, and the operation type Inventory → Configuration → Operation Types → Delivery Orders must have Generate Shipping Labels enabled.

The same steps work for the other carriers on your Neoship account: create a separate delivery method for each, as in chapter 02.

04

Packeta: a service per country

Packeta is set up differently from the other carriers: home delivery has a separate service for each country. After you choose Packeta as the carrier, a second picker asks for that service.

  1. On the delivery method, click Choose beside Neoship Carrier and select Packeta. A second picker, Packeta Home Delivery Carrier, lists a service per country: the CZ row a (CZ Zásilkovna domů HD, Czech Republic, CZK) for Czechia and the SK Packeta Home HD row b for Slovakia. Click Select c for the country this method delivers to. If you close the second picker without choosing, Packeta is not saved; click Choose again.
  2. Create a separate method for each country (here “Packeta – doručenie domov SK” and “… CZ”). Its Neoship Configuration a then stores Packeta as carrier b, the service chosen with the second Choose c and its country d. A Czech recipient on the Slovak method is refused before the shipment is created. Switching Test / Production clears the choice; choose again in the target environment.
05

Delivery to a pickup point

Instead of the customer's address, a shipment can go to a pickup point such as a Z-BOX. Carriers with Pickup Points ticked in the carrier list (chapter 02, step 4) support it; on the test account GLS, Packeta, SPS and Slovenská pošta.

The e-shop integration fills in the pickup point ID. It is the standard identifier each carrier uses for its pickup points (for example the Z-BOX number at Packeta), so the e-shop knows it from the pickup-point selection at checkout. You enter it by hand only for testing or an exceptional order, as the steps below show with Packeta.

  1. Find the pickup point in the Neoship list: aplikacia.neoship.sk/neoship/carriers/parcelshop/ (test environment: t-we-nshp-webapp-01-app.azurewebsites.net/neoship/carriers/parcelshop/). The carrier's identifier is the external ID; do not enter the internal ID of the row. For Z-BOX Žilina, Nám. Andreja Hlinku 7B (OD Mirage) it is 19407 (internal ID 40283).
  2. On the sales order, open Other Info a and enter the external ID in Neoship Pickup Point ID b. While the field is empty, the shipment goes to the customer's address.
  3. The order needs a delivery method of a carrier that supports pickup points; in the example “Packeta – doručenie domov SK”. Confirm the order and validate the delivery as in chapter 03. Only the pickup point ID decides where the shipment goes, so do not overwrite the customer's address, and for Packeta it does not matter which country the method is set up for.
  4. The label shows the pickup point as destination a, the routing strip contains ID 19407 b and the COD c of €94.50 is collected at the point (tracking Z2765212406).
06

Several boxes & partial deliveries

Several boxes in one delivery

  1. In Inventory → Configuration → Settings, enable Packages. You only do this once.
  2. Create and confirm an order with Neoship shipping as in chapter 03 (steps 1–4) and open its delivery. Do not validate it yet.
  3. On the delivery's Operations tab, click Details a on a line and set Destination Package: type a new name such as “Krabica 1/2” and create it. Each outer box b becomes one Neoship parcel; a box nested in another box does not add one. Pack all products or none: Odoo refuses a delivery with some products packed and some loose. Packages c lists the boxes of the delivery.
  4. Validate the delivery and print every label in the PDF and put one on each box: box 1 of 2 a and box 2 of 2 b, each with its own tracking number. The delivery's tracking reference lists all of them, separated by commas.
  5. To follow a single box, open it with the delivery's Packages button: it shows its own parcel with tracking number a, the reference with suffix _2 for the second box b and status c. The delivery shows the combined status.

Cancelling the SPS main package also cancels its child parcels; for Packeta, every box's shipment is cancelled.

Two deliveries from one COD order

The café Kaviareň Pod Hradom ordered six 1 kg bags at €34.90: €213.30 including €3.90 shipping, tagged Dobierka (order S00014). We dispatched three bags first and let Odoo create a backorder for the rest. Each delivery collects only what it delivers; nothing needs to be entered by hand.

  1. Validate the first partial delivery and choose Create Backorder. BA/OUT/00013 collects three bags and the shipping line: 3 × €34.90 + €3.90 = €108.60.
  2. Validate the backorder when the rest is ready. BA/OUT/00014 completes the order and collects the rest of the order total: €104.70. Together the two deliveries collect exactly €213.30.
  3. Check the amounts with the order's Neoship smart button: one parcel per delivery c, the first delivery a and the backorder b.

Sending the same delivery again (after a correction or a cancelled shipment) computes the same amount. Only validated deliveries are deducted, so a backorder still waiting in the warehouse does not change the amount of the current delivery.

Manual amount

If the customer paid part in advance or you agreed on a different split, open Additional Info a before sending (or after cancelling the shipment, chapter 09), tick Manual Cash on Delivery b and enter the amount c; 0.00 sends the delivery without COD. The manual amount cannot be negative, becomes read-only once the shipment exists and applies to this delivery only: the other deliveries still deduct its computed share.

Cases worth knowing

Three situations from everyday e-shop practice.

Personal pickup first, courier for the rest

Zuzana Horváthová ordered four 1 kg bags with SPS shipping, €143.50 COD (order S00019). She picked up two bags in the shop: on BA/OUT/00019 we switched the carrier to Osobný odber – Bratislava before validating, and on the backorder BA/OUT/00022 back to Kuriér SPS.

The backorder a goes by Kuriér SPS b and collects only its two bags, €69.80 c. The shipping line belongs to the first validated delivery whatever its carrier, so it was due at the pickup together with the first two bags (€73.70). If the courier should collect the shipping instead, enter the courier's amount manually.

A kit delivered in parts

Darčeková sada Ráno (€22.90) is a kit: a bill of materials of type Kit with 1 × Káva Etiópia 250 g and 1 × Hrnček Tatry. Jana Nováková ordered two sets with SPS shipping, €49.70 COD (order S00020). The delivery contains the components a, not the set, and only one mug was ready b: the first delivery holds one complete set and one extra coffee.

COD counts complete sets only: the first delivery a collects one set and the shipping, €26.80; the backorder b completes the second set and collects €22.90, which pays for the extra coffee too. Together €49.70.

Kits need Manufacturing and Sales; the bridge module Neoship Shipping - Kits installs automatically with them. Without it, Odoo does not compute COD for a kit delivered as components and asks for a manual amount.

A discount larger than the first delivery

Tomáš Šimko ordered three mugs with SPS shipping and got a fixed €20 discount through the order's Discount button a: €22.60 COD b (order S00021). Like shipping, the discount is a service line, so it belongs to the first delivery. The first delivery shipped one mug: €12.90 + €3.90 − €20.00 = −€3.20.

A parcel cannot collect a negative amount: the first delivery a goes without COD and the €3.20 is deducted from the next delivery b, which collects €25.80 − €3.20 = €22.60, exactly the order total. Nothing has to be entered by hand. Odoo asks for a manual amount only if the delivery completing the order would be negative, that is when the discounts exceed everything still to be collected.

07

Correct the data & retry safely

If Odoo or the carrier refuses a shipment, read the dialog, correct the data and validate the same delivery again. Do not create another sales order just to retry shipping.

ExampleAction
Missing ZIP, email or phoneComplete the contact. Odoo lists the missing fields (e.g. “Recipient … is missing: ZIP, email, phone”) before calling Neoship.
Packeta box over 15 kg, or no positive weightSplit the goods into boxes of at most 15 kg, or correct Weight for shipping. Neoship checks each box against the carrier's weight limit and refuses the whole delivery, so nothing is created.
Some products in boxes, others loosePut all products in boxes, or none.
CZ recipient on the SK Packeta home methodSelect the CZ home method. Do not change the recipient's real country to make the request pass.
COD of the delivery completing the order would be negative, because the discounts exceed the rest of the orderSet Manual Cash on Delivery on the delivery and enter the amount to collect.
COD currency or country not allowedAgree the payment arrangement or ask Neoship to configure the account; then retry the same delivery.
Pickup point not foundSelect a current, valid carrier-facing pickup ID.
Carrier not in the account price listUse an enabled method or ask Neoship to add the carrier to the account.
Timeout / uncertain resultWait a minute, then validate the same delivery again. The module first looks up the stable reference and reuses an existing matching shipment.

Example: a Packeta box over 15 kg

  1. Fifteen 1 kg bags weigh 15.75 kg packed. Neoship refuses the weight and creates nothing a; click Close b.
  2. Split the goods into boxes of at most 15 kg: in the coffee line's Details, put 8 bags in Krabica 1/2 a and add a line with the remaining 7 bags in Krabica 2/2 b. In Odoo 19, Add a line first asks for the source location (BA/Stock). Save c and validate the same delivery again: it went out as two Packeta shipments of 8.40 kg and 7.35 kg.

Accepted does not always mean valid. The module checks that a phone number is present and adds the country prefix to a local number, but does not validate the number. Verify contacts before dispatch; GLS may reject numbers that SPS accepts.

Why retrying normally does not create a duplicate

The stable reference is the order number with the number of the delivery, for example S00012-01 for the first delivery of order S00012 and S00012-02 for the next one. A delivery without a sales order uses its own name, for example BA-OUT-00006-01. In a multi-company database the reference gets a company prefix such as C1-. Once a delivery has been sent, its reference does not change. Before creating a shipment, the module looks for that reference. It reuses a matching printed shipment, cancels and replaces a shipment whose data changed, or deletes an unprinted rejected package before retrying. Several printed shipments with the same reference require manual review: Neoship does not guarantee unique references. If cancellation is refused, stop and investigate instead of forcing a replacement.

08

Labels, tracking & support's daily view

  1. On the sales order, click the Neoship smart button. It lists the order's Neoship parcels with the delivery, tracking number a, delivery status b (separate from the warehouse state) and the COD actually sent c. Cancelled shipments disappear from the list.
  2. Click Label d to open the stored PDF, or Tracking e to open the Neoship tracking page (test environment: t-we-nshp-webapp-01-app.azurewebsites.net/tracking/<number>/).
  3. If the label is missing or needs reprinting, open the delivery's Additional Info → Download Label Again. It fetches the label of the same shipment and attaches it again; the tracking number stays unchanged.
  4. Odoo reads the Neoship status right after sending, so a new shipment has it from the start (usually Exported). The scheduled job imports later status changes; click Update Tracking for an immediate refresh, also when the status could not be read at sending.
  5. In Inventory → Operations → Deliveries, open the search options a. The Neoship filters b select deliveries by carrier delivery status, not by stock state: Neoship: Not Delivered and Neoship: Returned c find problems, and Group By → Neoship Delivery d groups the list by delivery status.

Done ≠ delivered. Odoo's Done records the completed stock transfer. Neoship separately reports New, Exported, In Transit, Delivered, Not Delivered, Returned or Cancelled. A newly printed label is therefore Done in Odoo and Exported in Neoship. For several boxes, the delivery shows a combined status and each box keeps its own.

The scheduled job Neoship: Update Tracking runs every three hours, nominally at :30 UTC, in batches of 100 and at most 500 parcels per run; a parcel synced within the last hour is skipped. Tracking stops at Delivered / Returned / Cancelled, or after 30 days without a status change (system parameter delivery_neoship.tracking_days).

09

Cancel the shipment, not the stock history

  1. Open the delivery's Additional Info a, click Cancel b below the Tracking Reference and confirm Odoo's “Cancelling a delivery may not be undoable” with Ok. This cancels the shipment, not the sales order. After a successful cancellation, the parcel rows c and the tracking reference disappear; for several boxes, all parcels are cancelled.
  2. Check the “Shipment … cancelled” note a in the chatter. The earlier PDF attachments b remain; do not reuse a cancelled label. The delivery stays Done c, because cancelling a shipment does not reverse the stock movement. The tracking reference d is empty and Send to Shipper e creates a new shipment with the same reference if you need one. Handle returns or stock corrections through the normal Odoo workflow.

If the carrier already has the parcel, Neoship refuses the cancellation. The module keeps that parcel with its tracking number and an error in the parcel list, shows a warning and posts a note on the delivery; resolve the physical shipment with Neoship support.

While any transfer of the order has a Neoship shipment, the order cannot be cancelled: Odoo lists the transfers with their tracking numbers and asks you to cancel the shipments first (Additional Info → Cancel on the transfer). A shipment whose cancellation Neoship refused does not block cancelling the order.

Neoship marks a cancelled package as “Stornovať” and renames its reference to X<reference>, so the same delivery can be sent again with its original reference.

10

Configure closures & find protocols

Account-wide operation. A closure includes all shipments of the Neoship account at that carrier, including packages created outside Odoo. Do not click Close Now or activate a schedule on a shared test account without its owner's agreement.

  1. As administrator, open Inventory → Configuration → Delivery → Neoship Closure Schedules, or the delivery method's Closure Schedules smart button.
  2. Choosing SPS, Packeta or Slovenská pošta on a method creates a default archived schedule: Monday–Friday at 15:00 in the user's timezone. Only one default is created per account, environment and carrier; another method with the same carrier, e.g. Packeta (CZ), does not create a new one.
  3. Read the account-wide warning a. Check the delivery method, collection days, time and timezone c; here Europe/Bratislava. Keep Active b switched off until the collection arrangement is approved.
  4. Activating a schedule computes its Next Run and triggers the shared Neoship: Closures job at that time (it also runs hourly as a fallback). A closure is skipped on unticked days or when the same account was closed in the last 10 minutes. Close Now (d in step 3) runs a manual closure of the whole account immediately. Packeta closes by date and includes packages since the last closure, up to 7 days back.
  5. Open Neoship Protocols under Inventory → Operations or Configuration, or Protocols (e in step 3) on the schedule. Each run is recorded as a row with the result Closed, Nothing to Close or Failed a; download the protocol PDF when the carrier returns one b. The Packeta protocol is a handover list of the shipments: reference, barcode, recipient and COD amount. A successful Slovenská pošta closure may have a package list without a PDF.
