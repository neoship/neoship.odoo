Neoship Shipping

Ship Odoo deliveries through your Neoship account

Validate a delivery and Odoo creates the Neoship shipment, attaches the carrier label and follows the parcel until it is delivered. One integration covers every carrier active on your Neoship account, such as SPS, Packeta, GLS, DPD and Slovenská pošta.

A Neoship account is required. Neoship is a shipping platform for Slovak and Czech shops. Carriers, prices and contracts are arranged with Neoship; the module uses the Neoship API with your Neoship username and password. Neoship provides separate test and production environments with separate credentials.
Labels & tracking

The PDF label is attached to the delivery, the tracking number is stored and the Neoship status is updated automatically.

Cash on delivery

A rule on the delivery method decides which orders are cash on delivery. Every delivery collects exactly what it delivers.

Pickup points, boxes & closures

Send to pickup points, split a delivery into several boxes and close carrier shipments with a handover protocol.

Connect your account

Create a delivery method with the provider Neoship, enter your Neoship username and password and click Test Connection. The Test Environment switch in the header selects the Neoship test or production API.

Each delivery method has its own Odoo price (fixed price or margins) and uses one Neoship carrier. Create one method per carrier you ship with.

Choose the carrier from a live list

Choose loads the carriers active on your Neoship account, with their pickup point support. Packeta home delivery has a separate service per country; a second picker selects it, and a recipient from another country is refused before anything is sent.

Label formats come from Neoship and differ per carrier: A4 sheets or label printer sizes.

Ship when you validate the delivery

  • Validating a delivery creates the Neoship shipment and attaches the label PDF to the chatter.
  • The weight comes from Weight for shipping, the product weights or the method's default weight, converted to kilograms.
  • The delivery shows the tracking number, the Neoship status and one row per parcel.
  • Download Label Again reprints the same shipment; Send to Shipper retries after a correction.
  • Missing recipient data is reported before Neoship is called, and Neoship errors are shown with readable field names.

Cash on delivery that adds up

A domain on the delivery method, for example an order tag set by your e-shop, marks the cash on delivery orders. The amount is computed from the sales order:

  • Partial deliveries and backorders collect only what they deliver; together they collect the order total.
  • Shipping and discount lines belong to the first delivery; a negative remainder moves to the next one.
  • The amount is converted to a currency Neoship collects for the destination country.
  • A manual amount can be set on a single delivery when the customer paid part in advance.

Pickup points and several boxes

Set the carrier's pickup point ID in Neoship Pickup Point ID on the sales order, usually filled in by your e-shop integration, and the shipment goes to the pickup point instead of the customer's address.

With Packages enabled, every outer box of a delivery becomes its own Neoship parcel with its own label and tracking number.

Tracking, retries and cancellation

  • A scheduled action imports status changes every three hours; Update Tracking refreshes a delivery on demand.
  • Filter and group deliveries by Neoship status to find parcels that were not delivered or returned.
  • Every delivery has a stable reference based on the order number. A retry after a timeout reuses the existing shipment instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Cancel cancels the shipment at Neoship and keeps the stock history; an order with an active shipment cannot be cancelled by mistake.

Closures and protocols

For SPS, Packeta and Slovenská pošta, closure schedules close the day's shipments at the times you choose, and Close Now closes them immediately. Each run is recorded with its result and the carrier's protocol PDF.

A closure includes all shipments of the Neoship account at that carrier.

Data sent to Neoship

The module contacts Neoship only for delivery methods with the provider Neoship, using the credentials you enter on them. It sends:

  • your Neoship username and password, to sign in;
  • the sender's and recipient's name, company, street, city, ZIP, country, email and phone of each shipped delivery;
  • the shipment reference, weight, packages, pickup point ID and the cash on delivery amount, currency and reference.

It reads carriers, label formats, shipment statuses, labels and closure protocols from Neoship. Neoship processes this data as your shipping provider under its own terms and privacy policy, published on neoship.sk.
