Neoship Shipping - Kits

Cash on delivery for kits delivered as components

A bridge between Neoship Shipping and Manufacturing. It installs automatically when both Neoship Shipping and Sales with Manufacturing are installed.

Kits in partial deliveries

A product with a bill of materials of type Kit is delivered as its components. When only some components are ready, Neoship Shipping needs to know how many complete kits a delivery contains to compute its cash on delivery amount.

This module counts the complete kits from the delivered components. Each delivery collects the kits it completes, and the backorder collects the rest, so together the deliveries collect the order total.

Without it, a kit delivered as components needs a manual cash on delivery amount.

Good to know

  • Requires Neoship Shipping and a Neoship account.
  • Odoo 19; depends on Neoship Shipping and Sales - Manufacturing (sale_mrp).
